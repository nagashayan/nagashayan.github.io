About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Georgia State University, advised by Dr. Ashwin Ashok and Dr. Hemanth Venkateswara. My research focuses on developing intelligent assistive technologies for blind and low-vision individuals using artificial intelligence, computer vision, multimodal sensing, and edge computing.

Before beginning my PhD, I gained extensive industry experience in software engineering, cloud computing, and distributed systems. I currently work as a Senior Software Engineer at SiriusXM.

My broader research interests include machine learning, human-centered AI, and real-time intelligent systems that improve accessibility and human-computer interaction.

Research Interests

Assistive Artificial Intelligence | Computer Vision | Machine Learning | mmWave Radar | Edge AI | Human-Computer Interaction

Current Research

Real-Time Social Interaction Assistance

Developing AI-based systems to recognize social interaction cues, including handshake initiation, and provide real-time feedback to blind and low-vision users.

Multimodal Sensing for Assistive Navigation

Exploring computer vision and mmWave radar for wearable assistive systems capable of detecting environmental obstacles and supporting navigation in unfamiliar environments.
